About Our Institute

Jashore Government Polytechnic Institute

Established under the Directorate of Technical Education and affiliated with the Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB), Jashore Government Polytechnic Institute (JPI) stands as one of the premier technical education centers in Bangladesh. We offer 4-year Diploma in Engineering degrees across 7 core technological disciplines.

Campus Location Jashore, Khulna Division, Bangladesh
